What Vynta does

Built around how you actually work

🧠

Natural Language Input

Just describe your task the way you'd say it out loud. "Submit the report by Thursday" is enough. No forms, no date pickers.

⚡

Energy-Aware Scheduling

Pick Low, Medium, or High energy. Vynta slots your task into the right part of your day — not just the next open gap.

📅

Google Calendar Sync

Two-way sync with your existing calendar. Tasks show up where you already look. No double-booking, no duplicates.

🛡️

Focus Shield

Start a deep work session and Vynta activates Do Not Disturb automatically. Turns off when you mark it done.

🔄

Task Reflow

Missed something yesterday? One tap reschedules it into today's open slots. No manual rebuilding.

📊

Productivity History

See your full task log with a daily productivity score. Understand your patterns, not just your to-do list.

How it works

Three steps.
Your whole day planned.

01

Describe your task

Type or speak naturally. "Finish the ML assignment before Sunday" is all it needs.

02

Pick your energy level

Low, Medium, or High. Tells the AI how much mental effort this task needs.

03

Let AI schedule it

Vynta finds the right slot in your calendar, explains why it chose that time, and adds it instantly.
